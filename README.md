# Njabulisto Digitals

Websites that bring bookings for guesthouses and local businesses in Gqeberha.

## What's in here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Your business website, with a savings calculator, packages and a WhatsApp contact form |
| `demo/guesthouse/index.html` | A sample guesthouse website to show prospects. Change it for each one in minutes |
| `sales/scripts.md` | Word-for-word scripts for walk-ins and WhatsApp, plus answers for when people say no |
| `sales/price-sheet.html` | One-page price sheet. Open it, tap **Save as PDF**, send it on WhatsApp |
| `sales/invoice.html` | Editable invoice with automatic totals. Tap text to edit, then **Save as PDF** |

## Before you use anything: fill in your details

1. **`index.html`**: search for `WHATSAPP_NUMBER` and `EMAIL` near the bottom. Put in your real number, like `27821234567` (no `+`, no spaces, no leading 0).
2. **`sales/price-sheet.html`**: replace `0XX XXX XXXX` and the email at the bottom.
3. **`sales/invoice.html`**: replace your name, bank details and contact details (or just tap and edit them on screen each time).

## Making a mockup for a guesthouse (about 15 minutes)

1. Copy the `demo/guesthouse` folder and rename it, e.g. `demo/ocean-bay`.
2. Open `index.html` in that folder and edit **only the `SITE` block** near the bottom: name, rooms, prices and WhatsApp.
3. Optional: add their photos to the same folder and write the file names in `heroPhoto`, `photo` and so on. Real photos make the mockup far more convincing.
4. Keep the "demo website" ribbon at the top until they've paid. Delete that line when it goes live.

## Putting your site live for free (GitHub Pages)

1. On GitHub, open this repository → **Settings** → **Pages**.
2. Under "Branch", choose your main branch and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
3. After a minute or two, your site is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.
4. Later, buy `njabulisto.co.za` (about R100–R250 a year) and connect it under the same Pages settings.
