Questions

Straight answers.

Do I have to leave Booking.com or Airbnb?

No. Keep them for new guests who find you there. Your website catches the guests who already know you, return guests and people who search for you on Google, so those bookings come to you without commission.

How do bookings come through?

Guests pick their dates, room and number of people, then tap "Book". It opens a ready-written WhatsApp message to you. You confirm availability and payment the way you already do. No new system to learn.

What do I need to give you?

Your room photos, room names and prices, and a few lines about your place. If your photos aren't great, I'll tell you honestly.

What if I already have a website?

Send me the link. If it's slow, hard to use on a phone, or doesn't let people book easily, I'll show you a better version for free and you can decide.

Are there monthly fees?

The once-off price covers the build and your first year of domain. The Care Plan (R350/month) is optional and covers hosting, updates and changes. Cancel anytime.
