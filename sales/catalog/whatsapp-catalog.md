# WhatsApp Business Catalog: copy and paste

Pictures are in `assets/whatsapp-catalog/`. Save them to your phone first.

## How to add an item (about 2 minutes each)

1. Open **WhatsApp Business** → tap **⋮** (or **Settings** on iPhone) → **Business tools** → **Catalog**.
2. Tap **Add new item**.
3. Add the picture, then copy each field below.
4. Tap **Save**. Repeat for all 5, **in this order**, so Free Mockup shows first.

## Tips
- **Share the whole catalog** with a prospect: open a chat → tap 📎 → **Catalog** → send. Or share one item, e.g. Book Direct, after a walk-in.
- **Set a greeting message** (Business tools → Greeting message):
  > Hi 👋 Thanks for contacting Njabulisto Digitals. I build websites that bring guesthouses bookings without commission. Want a free mockup of your website? Just reply with your business name.
- **Set an away message** for after hours, so nobody waits in silence.
- If you change a price, change it in the catalog, on the website and on the price sheet. Same price everywhere.
