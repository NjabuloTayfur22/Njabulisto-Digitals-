# Njabulisto Digitals: Sales Scripts

Use these word for word at first. Once they feel natural, make them your own.

**The golden rule:** talk about **their money**, not your design. Owners don't buy websites. They buy more bookings and less commission.

---

## Before you walk in (10 minutes per guesthouse)

1. Google the guesthouse name on your phone. Screenshot what comes up first. Is it Booking.com or Airbnb, or their own site?
2. Check their Google Business Profile. Are the photos good? Is it missing hours? How many reviews?
3. Open the demo site (`demo/guesthouse/`), change the name and rooms to theirs, and save it.
4. Write down **one specific problem** you found, e.g. "No website, only Booking.com shows up" or "Website doesn't work on a phone."

**Never walk in without a mockup that has their name on it.** That's what makes you different from everyone else who pitches them.

---

## Script 1: Walk-in (the main one)

**Best times:** 10:00–11:30 or 14:00–16:00. Avoid breakfast (07:00–09:30) and check-in time.

> **You:** "Morning! Is the owner or manager around? I'm [your name], I run a small web business here in Summerstrand. It'll take two minutes."

*(If the owner isn't there: "No problem. When's a good time to catch them?" Write it down and come back. Don't pitch the cleaner or the receptionist.)*

> **You:** "Quick question. When guests Google your guesthouse, do you know what comes up first?"

*(Let them answer. Then show your screenshot.)*

> **You:** "This is what I see. The first thing is Booking.com. So even guests who already know you end up booking through the app, and you pay commission on them, usually around 15%."

> **You:** "So I built you this."

*(Hand them your phone with the mockup open. **Stop talking.** Let them scroll. Silence is your friend here.)*

> **You:** "It's your own website. Guests pick their dates and it sends the booking straight to your WhatsApp. No commission. You keep Booking.com for new guests, and this catches the returning ones and the Google searchers."

> **You:** "It's R4,500 once-off, live in 7 days. You pay half to start, and the other half only when you've seen it live and you're happy. Want me to set it up?"

**Then stop talking and wait.** Whoever speaks first after the price usually loses.

---

## Script 2: WhatsApp first message (after a walk-in, or cold)

> Hi [Name], this is [your name] from Njabulisto Digitals in Summerstrand 👋
>
> I noticed when people Google [Guesthouse Name], the first result is Booking.com, so even your regular guests probably book through the app and you pay commission on them.
>
> I made a quick mockup of what your own booking website could look like: [link]
>
> Bookings go straight to your WhatsApp, no commission. Happy to pop in for 5 minutes and show you. When suits you?

**Rules:**
- Send it with a **voice note** under 30 seconds. People trust a voice.
- No reply after 3 days? Send **one** follow-up: *"Hi [Name], just checking you saw the mockup? No pressure at all."*
- No reply after that? Move on. Come back in a month.

---

## Script 3: Follow-up after "Let me think about it"

> Hi [Name], thanks for your time yesterday. I've kept your mockup live here: [link]
>
> Just so it's easy: R2,250 to start, the rest only when you're happy with it. I've got space for 2 more guesthouses this month. Should I hold one for you?

Only say "space for 2 more" **if it's true.** Never lie to create pressure. If they find out, you lose them and everyone they talk to.

## Answers to what they'll say

### "It's too expensive."
> "I hear you. Let's check quickly: what do you charge a night, roughly? *(e.g. R1,200)* At 15%, that's R180 to the app on every night booked. If this site moves about 25 nights a year to direct, it's paid for itself. Most guesthouses get that from returning guests alone."

If they still can't: offer **Get Found (R1,500)** as a smaller first step. **Don't** cut the price of Book Direct.

### "I already have a website."
> "Great, can I see it?" *(Open it on your phone in front of them.)* "When a guest opens it on a phone, can they book in under a minute?"

If it's slow or old: "I'll do a free mockup of an updated version. If it's not clearly better, you've lost nothing."
If it's actually good: say so honestly, and offer Get Found or the Care Plan instead.

### "My nephew / a friend does my website."
> "No problem at all. If it ever gets stuck or he gets busy, here's my number." *(Leave a card. Move on politely.)*

### "Booking.com brings me all my guests. I don't need this."
> "Definitely keep Booking.com. It's great for new guests. This is just for the guests who **already** know you. Right now they have no way to book you directly, so you pay commission on guests you earned yourself."

### "I need to talk to my partner."
> "Of course. Can I send you the mockup on WhatsApp so you can both look at it tonight?" *(Get their number. Follow up in 2 days.)*

### "How do I know you'll finish it?"
> "That's exactly why you only pay half to start. If I don't deliver, you've only paid half. And I'm right here in Summerstrand, so you know where to find me."

### "Can you do it cheaper?"
> "I'd rather not lower the price, but I can make it smaller. Get Found is R1,500 and gets you showing up properly on Google. We can add the website later."

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't use tech words (SEO, responsive, hosting, CMS, domain propagation). Say "shows up on Google", "works on phones" and "your own web address".
- ❌ Don't promise a number of bookings. Promise what you control: the website, delivered in 7 days.
- ❌ Don't trash Booking.com. Owners rely on it. You're adding, not replacing.
- ❌ Don't start work without the 50% deposit in your account.
- ❌ Don't use a real guesthouse's name or photos on a public website before they've paid. Keep their mockup private (send the link only to them).
